Buy It For Life

Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

Buy It For Life is an app for tracking the clothes you own and what they cost per wear. It is built to keep your information private: there is no account, no server, no advertising and no tracking.

The short version

  • We do not collect any data about you.
  • Your wardrobe is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. We cannot see it.
  • Your location is only used to get the weather forecast, and only if you allow it.

Your wardrobe data

The items you add (names, photos, prices, dates, notes, what you wore on which day) and your settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they are also synced through your own private iCloud database so they appear on your other devices. This data is protected by your Apple Account. The developer has no access to it and runs no server that receives it.

Photos and camera

The app only uses photos you take with the camera or choose from your library for an item. Cutting a garment out of its background happens on your iPhone. Photos are stored with your wardrobe as described above.

Location and weather

If you choose “current location”, the app asks for permission to use your location while the app is open. It asks for an approximate location by default, which is accurate to a few kilometres. If you choose a city instead, no location permission is needed.

To show the forecast, the app sends that approximate location, or your chosen city’s coordinates, to Apple’s weather service (WeatherKit). Apple’s handling of that request is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. The last known location is kept on your device so the forecast can be shown when you are offline. It is never sent to the developer.

Weather data is provided by Apple Weather. Data sources.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that collects data. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Deleting your data

You can delete any item in the app. To delete everything, delete the app and remove its iCloud data in the Settings app under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Buy It For Life.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes
