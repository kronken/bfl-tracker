Common questions

How is cost per wear calculated?

What you paid, minus what you sold it for, divided by the number of times you’ve worn it. That includes your “worn so far” estimate from before you started using the app.

Why don’t I see the weather?

Choose a city, or allow location, in the app’s Settings tab. If you chose current location, check that location access is allowed for Buy It For Life in the iPhone Settings app.

Is my wardrobe backed up?

Yes, if iCloud is turned on for the app. Your data is stored in your own iCloud and syncs to your other devices. Nobody else can see it.

How do I delete my data?

Delete items in the app, or delete the app and remove its iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
