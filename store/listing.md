## Description (max 4000)

Know what every piece costs, per wear.

Good boots, a well-made jacket, jeans you wear for years: things that are built to last cost more up front and less over time. Buy It For Life shows you exactly how much less.

REGISTER WHAT YOU OWN
Add each item with a photo, what you paid, when you bought it and the weather it's for. Photos can be cut out from their background automatically, right on your iPhone.

TAP WHAT YOU WEAR
Once a day, tap what you're wearing. Today shows the forecast where you are and puts the clothes that suit the weather first, so the rain shell comes up when it's raining and the sandals wait for summer. Forgot a day? Open the calendar and log it afterwards.

WATCH THE COST FALL
• Cost per wear for every item, falling with each wear
• A "Paid off" mark when an item drops under your own target
• Wardrobe cost per day, year by year
• What you spent each year, and what you got back from sales
• Items you haven't worn in months, so you know what to wear or sell

SELL WITH THE FULL PICTURE
Mark an item as sold and the sale price comes off its cost. Its history stays, so your numbers stay honest.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your wardrobe is stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, and nobody else can see it. Your location is only used to fetch the weather.

## App Review notes

No login is needed. On first launch the app shows a short onboarding: pick a city (or allow approximate location) for the weather forecast, then add a first item. Weather data comes from Apple WeatherKit. All data is stored on device and in the user's private CloudKit database; the app has no server.

## App Privacy questionnaire

Data collection: **No, we do not collect data from this app.**
(Weather requests send an approximate location to Apple WeatherKit; neither the developer nor third parties receive it. Wardrobe data lives only on the device and in the user's private iCloud.)
